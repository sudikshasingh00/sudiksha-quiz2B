Meet the Herd

Every animal at Dusty Toes came to us needing a second chance. Here are six of the residents you might meet on a farm visit.

Sponsorship spaces for this season are now open. A sponsorship covers feed, shearing and vet checks for one animal for a full year. Sponsors receive a photo update every month, an invitation to the spring open day, and a certificate with their animal's name. Spaces are limited, so get in touch soon if you would like to help.

Biscuit

A shy alpaca who warms up quickly if you bring carrots.

Pepper

Our oldest llama and the unofficial guard of the gate.

Juniper

Arrived last spring and has not stopped exploring since.

Moss

Loves a dust bath more than any animal on the farm.

Clover

Always first to the fence when visitors arrive.

Tumble

Named for the way he greeted us on his very first day.
